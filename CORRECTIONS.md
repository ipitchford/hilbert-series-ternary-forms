# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to hilbert-series-ternary-septic-octic-nonic 0.2.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22978726)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.2.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/hilbert-series-ternary-septic-octic-nonic/>.

1. Corrected the lower bound in §6, “Minimal generators in low degree”, second bullet. The archived text takes the maximum of a_m + a_{n−m} − 1 over all 0 < m < n. Taken that way the bound can exceed the monomial count (for d = 7, n = 12, m = 3 it gives 12 against 6 monomials). The maximum should be taken only over m with a_m ≥ 1 and a_{n−m} ≥ 1.

   The published ranges (415–416 and 8233–8254) were computed with the restricted maximum and are correct. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
